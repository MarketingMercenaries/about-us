What a scoped engagement looks like.

We agree on the decision or question first, then the evidence, methods and deliverables. Primary interviews, surveys and additional markets are explicitly scoped rather than assumed.

Research and advisory may be delivered through a focused Strategic Sprint or a custom project. When the problem is specifically GTM, the paid GTM Diagnostic has its own defined baseline.

  • FrameDefine the decision, audiences, constraints and what would change the recommendation.
  • InvestigateCollect relevant market, customer, competitive and institutional evidence.
  • TestChallenge assumptions, surface counterevidence and label unresolved questions.
  • AdviseDeliver clear options, priorities, rationale and an action plan.
  • Carry throughWhen scoped, translate strategy into messaging, content and GTM execution.

Evidence before assertion.

Published and commissioned research should be useful because readers can understand how conclusions were reached — not because the language sounds certain.

Research integrity standard

We distinguish verified facts, interpretation and hypotheses; identify meaningful limitations; credit underlying sources; and disclose when analysis is commissioned. A client can commission a question, but does not purchase a predetermined finding. Public research and client-confidential research are handled separately.

For technology-related institutional and policy questions, see our Public Affairs & Issue Research practice.
