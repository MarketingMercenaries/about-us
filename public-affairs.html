Independent analysis. Transparent sponsorship.

The same research standard applies whether a question comes from a business, organization or a public-interest investigation.

How findings are handled

We distinguish source evidence from judgment, disclose commissioned work where published, note meaningful uncertainty and corrections, and do not promise a predetermined conclusion. Research and advisory engagements are scoped with clear terms, including confidentiality and publication rights where relevant.

This practice does not imply electoral campaign management, lobbying representation, legal advice or advocacy for a position the analysis has not supported.
