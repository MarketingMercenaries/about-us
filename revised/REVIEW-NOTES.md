## Open the site

Extract the ZIP and open `revised/index.html`. The portfolio is `revised/portfolio.html`; Zero to Market is `revised/blog.html`. Desktop and phone screenshots are in the separate `previews` folder.

For GitHub, upload the contents of `revised` into the existing website directory. Preserve the `assets` subfolder and all image filenames. Do not upload the ZIP itself or nest the website inside another `revised` folder. Merge robots.txt and sitemap.xml with any unrelated existing deployment rules.
